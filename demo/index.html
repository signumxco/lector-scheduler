Lector Scheduler

Automated Mass reading assignments — availability collection, schedule generation, lector emails, and swap requests, all running on tools your parish already has.

The Problem

Scheduling lectors is tedious

  • Coordinators email every lector individually to ask for availability — then wait for replies
  • Building the schedule by hand means cross-referencing a spreadsheet and hoping nobody double-books
  • Last-minute substitution requests come in by text or phone — then it's on the coordinator to scramble
  • Lectors have to look up their own readings — the coordinator doesn't have time to include them in the email
What it does

The whole cycle, automated

  • Availability collection — each lector gets a unique link to toggle which Masses they can serve that month
  • Auto-schedule generation — balances load across lectors, prevents double-booking, flags gaps for the coordinator
  • Assignment emails — lectors receive their dates, full reading texts (from USCCB.org), and prep notes
  • Self-service swaps — if a lector can't serve, one click broadcasts to the whole lector pool; first to respond takes the slot

Self-service substitutions

Need a sub? One click does the work.

  • Lector clicks "Request Swap" in their assignment email and selects the Mass they need covered
  • The system immediately emails all other active lectors: "Can you cover Sunday June 1 at 9:00 AM?"
  • First lector to reply is assigned to that Mass — the coordinator doesn't have to do anything
  • Swap tokens never expire — lectors can request coverage anytime during the month, not just at the start

Built-in liturgical awareness

Details that matter to parishes

  • Saturday Vigil readings are automatic — the scheduler knows evening Masses use the next Sunday's readings, with no configuration needed
  • Special Masses are supported — add one-time Masses for Triduum, Christmas, Ash Wednesday, school Masses, etc. in the MassTimes tab
  • Scripture is always authoritative — reading texts are scraped verbatim from USCCB.org, never AI-generated or paraphrased
  • Variable lector counts — weekday Masses can have 1 lector, Sundays 2, special events more. Set per Mass in the sheet.
  • Your data, your account — everything stays in the parish's own Google account. Nothing is shared with any third party.

One-time setup

5 steps to go live

1
Copy the Google Sheet template
Make a copy of the template Sheet into your parish's Google Drive. Note the Spreadsheet ID from the URL.
2
Paste and deploy the Apps Script
Open Extensions → Apps Script in your Sheet. Paste in Code.gs. Deploy as a web app. Authorize with your Google account.
3
Add your lectors and Mass times
Fill in the Lectors tab (name + email) and MassTimes tab (day, time, label). Add any recurring or one-time special Masses.
4
Fork the repo and enable GitHub Pages
Fork the lector-scheduler GitHub repository. Enable Pages on the main branch. Update the Apps Script URL in the two web form files.
5
Set the 5 config values and create triggers
In Script Properties: SPREADSHEET_ID, COORDINATOR_EMAIL, GITHUB_USERNAME, PARISH_NAME, SCRIPT_URL. Then use the menu to set up monthly triggers.
